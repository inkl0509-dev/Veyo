Privacy Policy

ARA IRI Service · Last updated 12 November 2026

Who we are. ARA IRI Service ("we") provides air-conditioning installation, repair and maintenance in the Maldives. Our apps are ARA IRI Service (customers), ARA IRI Tech (our technicians) and ARA IRI Office (our staff).

Contact: araairiservice@gmail.com · Phone: +960 7796595

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and, if you give it, your TIN.
  • Service address and location: the address and island you enter, and the map location you choose, so our technician can find you.
  • Service details: what's wrong with your AC, notes and photos you add, service history, quotations, invoices, payments recorded and service reports.
  • Messages you send to our office or technician in the app.
  • Notification token: a device code used only to send you notifications about your service.
  • Technicians only: while a technician is travelling to a job, the ARA IRI Tech app shares their location with the office and the customer for that job. It stops when the trip ends.

We do not collect payment card details, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To book, schedule and carry out your service, and to contact you about it.
  • To send quotations, invoices, service reports and reminders.
  • To keep our accounts and meet legal and tax requirements.

Who can see it

Only our office staff and the technician assigned to your job. Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which processes it on our behalf. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is protected by sign-in and access rules.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and service history while you use our service. Invoices and payment records are kept as long as required for accounting and tax purposes.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can view and edit your details in the app under your profile.
  • You can use the customer app without an account (guest booking).
  • To delete your account and data, email or call us using the contact details above. We will delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
  • You can turn off notifications and location permission at any time in your phone settings.

Children

Our service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above.
